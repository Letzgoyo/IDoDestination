# Cancellations and refunds

**Last updated:** [[date]]

This policy applies to bookings paid through I Do Destination. It sits alongside the Australian Consumer Law, and nothing here removes a right you have under it.

## If you cancel

| When you cancel | Refund |
|---|---|
| Within 48 hours of paying (not trials) | 100% |
| 60 or more days before your wedding | 100% |
| 30 to 59 days before your wedding | 50% |
| Fewer than 30 days before your wedding | No refund |
| A paid trial | No refund |
| A request the vendor has not yet been paid for | No cost, cancel any time |

If you did not give a wedding date, only the 48-hour rule applies.

## If the vendor cancels

You always receive a full refund.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, in AUD, usually within 5 to 10 business days. Your bank may take longer to show it.

## How to cancel

Open your private booking link (sent to you by email) and choose **Cancel booking**. It shows the refund you will receive before you confirm.

## Problems with a service

If a vendor did not provide the service as agreed, contact us at [[contact email]] within [[14]] days and we will review it. We may refund you in whole or in part.

---

*The percentages and day counts above are set in the site configuration (`REFUND_FULL_DAYS`, `REFUND_PARTIAL_DAYS`) and the 48-hour and trial rules in `server/bookings.js`. If you change them there, update this document.*
