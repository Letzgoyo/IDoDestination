# Privacy policy

**Last updated:** [[date]]

[[Business legal name]] (ABN [[ABN]]) ("we", "us") operates I Do Destination. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

## What we collect

**Couples**
- Name and email address
- Wedding date, guest numbers and the messages you send to vendors
- Booking details (service, price, status) and payment confirmations. Card details are collected and processed by Stripe and are not stored by us.

**Vendors**
- Business and contact details (name, email, phone, website, Instagram)
- Business description, services and prices, and the photos you upload
- Payout details, which are collected and held by Stripe, not by us

**Everyone**
- Basic technical information needed to run and secure the Site, such as IP address (used for rate limiting and abuse prevention).

## Why we collect it

- To run the marketplace: show vendors, pass on enquiries and booking requests, take payments and pay vendors
- To send emails about your booking or application (confirmations, approvals, cancellations and refunds)
- To review vendor applications
- To keep the Site secure, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations

We do not sell personal information.

## Who we share it with

- **The vendor you book or contact.** They receive your name, email, wedding date, guest numbers and message. Vendors may be located overseas, so your details may be disclosed outside Australia.
- **Stripe**, to process payments and vendor payouts (Stripe may process data in the United States and other countries).
- **Our email provider** ([[email provider]]) to send emails.
- **Our hosting provider** ([[hosting provider]]) to store the Site's data.
- **OpenStreetMap tile servers**, which receive your IP address when the map loads.
- Authorities or advisers where the law requires or allows it.

When we send your information to an overseas recipient we take reasonable steps to make sure they handle it appropriately.

## Keeping it safe and how long we keep it

We use access controls, private booking links and encrypted connections. We keep booking and payment records for as long as the law requires (generally seven years for financial records) and other information only as long as we need it. If we become aware of a data breach likely to cause serious harm we will notify affected people and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required.

## Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted (subject to our legal record-keeping duties), by emailing [[privacy contact email]]. We will respond within a reasonable time, usually 30 days.

## Complaints

Please contact us first at [[privacy contact email]]. If you are not satisfied you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
